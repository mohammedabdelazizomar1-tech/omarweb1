# ERP System Roadmap & Milestones

Future optimization and enhancement milestones.

## Phase 1: Core Automation (Completed)
- Clean Architecture implementation.
- Dynamic partner share calculation and quota tracker views.
- Arabic multi-sheet Excel importer integrations.

- Mobile-responsive boarding passenger check-in.

## Phase 3: AI Analytics & Forecasting (Q4 2026)
- AI-driven flight and hotel price predictive analysis.
- Multi-tenant cloud-hosted SaaS deployment architecture.
